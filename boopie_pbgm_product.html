Our Story

Born from Love, Built for Comfort.

The idea for Boopie didn't come from a boardroom; it came from a living room, much like yours. When our founder's beloved rescue cat, Barnaby, was diagnosed with diabetes, the daily blood sugar checks became a source of dread for both of them.

Traditional monitors were clunky, the lancets were intimidating, and the stress was taking a toll on their bond. We searched for a better way—a device that understood the sensitive nature of cats and the anxiety of their owners. When we couldn't find it, we decided to create it.

"Our mission is simple: to empower you to manage your cat's health with confidence, compassion, and ease. We believe that caring for a diabetic cat should strengthen your bond, not strain it."

The Problem: Loud clicks and deep pricks startle cats, making testing a struggle.

The Boopie Solution: Our ultra-fine, precision-engineered lancets require only a microscopic blood drop. The delivery mechanism is whisper-quiet, minimizing stress and discomfort.

Seamless Smart App Integration

Usage Guide

A Gentle Routine in 3 Simple Steps.

Designed to be quick, painless, and rewarding for both of you.

1

Prepare & Comfort

Find a quiet spot. Gently pet your cat and warm their ear to encourage blood flow. Insert a fresh test strip to auto-turn on the meter.

2

The Gentle Prick

Use the Boopie lancing device on the marginal ear vein. Our whisper-quiet mechanism ensures a nearly painless experience.

3

Test & Reward

Touch strip to the tiny blood drop. Results appear in 5s and sync to the app. Give your brave kitty a diabetes-friendly treat!

Best Practices for a Calm Cat

  • Associate the testing area with positive experiences (treats, cuddles) even when not testing.
  • Stay calm yourself; your cat is highly sensitive and can sense your anxiety.
  • Consistency is key—try to establish a routine by testing at the same times each day.

General Questions

Can Boopie be used for dogs too?

Yes! While Boopie was designed with the delicate nature of cats in mind, it is equally accurate and effective for monitoring dogs with diabetes.

Does it require coding?

No. Boopie features auto-coding technology, eliminating an extra step and reducing the chance of errors during stressful moments.

Usage & Accuracy

How much blood is needed for a test?

Boopie requires only a tiny 0.4µL (microliter) sample of blood. This microscopic amount makes the process much easier to achieve and less painful for your pet.

Are the readings the same as what my vet gets?

Boopie is calibrated specifically for the unique properties of feline and canine blood, providing highly accurate results comparable to laboratory testing.

App & Connectivity

Is the Boopie app free?

Yes, the Boopie app is completely free to download and use for both iOS and Android devices.

Can I share my cat's data with my vet?

Absolutely. The app allows you to export your pet's blood glucose history as a clean, easy-to-read PDF or CSV file directly to your veterinarian's email with just a tap.
